About Me

A background in education, a passion for data, and an interest in using analytical thinking to solve meaningful problems.

Hello, I'm Andrea.

My professional journey began in elementary education, where I developed a passion for helping others learn, grow, and succeed. Working across multiple grade levels taught me the importance of understanding individual needs, interpreting information, and communicating insights in ways that make a difference.

That same curiosity and problem-solving mindset led me to pursue data science. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Data Science from Bellevue University, building a foundation in programming, statistical analysis, data visualization, and machine learning.

I am excited by the ever-changing nature of data science. Each challenge brings something new to explore, learn, and solve. My goal is to combine analytical thinking, technical skills, and clear communication to turn data into meaningful insights.

My background

My Journey into Data Science

Before transitioning into data science, I worked in elementary education, teaching across multiple grade levels. My experience involved helping students develop foundational reading skills, evaluating student progress, and using assessment data to identify learning needs.

I also worked closely with families, communicating student performance and turning data into information they could understand and use. These experiences strengthened my ability to interpret information, recognize patterns, and explain complex concepts to nontechnical audiences.

I bring that same approach to data science: asking questions, exploring information, identifying patterns, and communicating findings in a way that supports informed decisions.
